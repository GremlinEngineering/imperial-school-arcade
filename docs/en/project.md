# Imperial School Arcade

The Imperial School Arcade is Dmitry Malykhin's autobiography built from data about play and told through retro games. Its principle is: **when I play, the website changes**.

The site connects game facts, personal results and the author's interpretation of that experience. A session may produce an observation, achievement or record. Accumulated experience can justify revisiting particular texts, relationships and recommendations, so earlier material continues to evolve.

## What readers can find

| Area | Reader's purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Catalogue and series | Discover games, versions, history and relationships |
| Personal profile and journal | Follow completion, achievements, results and changing interests |
| Recommendations and plans | Find a next game and a concrete first activity |
| Media library | View artwork and find/listen to music |
| Applications | Use music and image collections on devices |
| Artwork | See time, events and weather expressed through appearance and scenes |
| Laboratory | Run early-game reconstructions and reach their research |

Each area owns its purpose and rules. Shared games, material and events connect them while retaining provenance.

## From experience to material

Suppose an achievement is earned in a familiar game. The system records a confirmed change. Rules then check which material is affected: a personal description, summary, portrait or artistic state. An eligible task receives data and prepared context.

One event does not unconditionally recreate everything. Each material has its own revision basis. Publication presents a prepared result; opening a page does not compose new writing or artwork.

## Who establishes meaning

Dmitry defines the concept, rules, editorial questions and art direction. Algorithms and heuristics establish admissible relationships and conditions. Models help prepare text and images from those tasks. Profile observations, reference facts, authored notes and generated text retain distinct roles.

Gremlin is the author's gaming alter ego. Fox is Gremlin's companion. These characters belong to the project's artistic world; gameplay results are established by the relevant source data.

## Distinct terms

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Work, version, card | A game, its particular edition and its presentation in a site section |
| Personal result, recognition | A player's achievement versus a game's historical recognition in sources/lists |
| Site theme | Page appearance and its calendar |
| Wallpaper collection | Images and declared device formats |
| Dynamic device theme | Artwork and frame-composition rules for one screen composition |
| Weather source | Selection data, independent of artwork |
| Player, viewer, application | Music playback, image viewing and installed software are described separately |
