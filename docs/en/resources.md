## Data and materials

[RetroAchievements](https://retroachievements.org/) provides profiles, achievements/results; [MobyGames](https://www.mobygames.com/) edition facts; [Libretro Thumbnails](https://github.com/libretro-thumbnails/libretro-thumbnails) game images; [HowLongToBeat](https://howlongtobeat.com/) completion estimates; [Speedrun.com](https://www.speedrun.com/) competitive runs; [MARP](http://replay.marpirc.net/) arcade results. [GameFAQs](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/) and [Internet Archive](https://archive.org/) supply manuals/historical material when referenced by a particular publication.

Music sources include [SMS Power!](https://www.smspower.org/), [Zophar](https://www.zophar.net/), [VGMPF](https://www.vgmpf.com/) and [Games Database](https://www.gamesdatabase.org/). [Game Music Emu](https://github.com/libgme/game-music-emu) decodes native formats. Each publication retains its source/rights.

[Аuthoring components](sources.md) include [Natasha](https://github.com/natasha/natasha) and [pymorphy3](https://github.com/no-plagiarism/pymorphy3) inside the local entity resolver, [MiniSearch](https://github.com/lucaong/minisearch) for local search, [Cytoscape](https://js.cytoscape.org/) for the graph, [FullCalendar](https://fullcalendar.io/) for events, and [ReportLab](https://www.reportlab.com/) for PDF typesetting. Libraries implement specific operations; the Arcade supplies its own rules/relationships.

For corrections or research collaboration, include the page and passage in question. Application repositories remain independent: [GremlinAmp](https://github.com/GremlinEngineering/gremlinamp), [Imperial School Views](https://github.com/GremlinEngineering/imperial-school-views), [Tennis for Two](https://github.com/malyhind/tennis-for-two).
