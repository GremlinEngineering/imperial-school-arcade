# Navigation, accessibility and metadata

A material can be reached from the catalogue, search, library or a direct link. Its destination should retain its meaning across themes and screen sizes. Game links identify games; source-code links identify the corresponding project.

Controls need meaningful names and states. Music distinguishes play, pause, loading and errors. Changing continuation policy must not unexpectedly restart a track. Keyboard handling respects text-entry fields. Zoom, full viewing, readable mobile captions and reachable controls serve different needs; a larger image does not replace text or an unavailability message.

Semantic markup and linked data describe distinct entities such as games, authors, music and sources where provided. Machine metadata does not create evidence or grant rights. Link-preview artwork should identify its page. These are intended behaviours, not a claim of independent accessibility certification.
