# Sources and methods

Game facts link to their reference sources and results. Authorial views remain authored writing. Artistic weather expressions are not physical measurements of seawater.

## Methods

- [Game Music Emu](https://github.com/libgme/game-music-emu): emulated game sound systems.
- [emu2413](https://github.com/digital-sound-antiques/emu2413): YM2413 emulation through the shared GremlinAmp implementation.
- SciPy [STFT](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.signal.stft.html), [DCT](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.fft.dct.html), and [cKDTree](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.spatial.cKDTree.html): acoustic-analysis operations.
- [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs): weather data and field definitions.
- [Astral](https://astral.readthedocs.io/): solar events and Moon information.
- [Fantasy Calendar](https://github.com/fantasycalendar/Fantasy-Calendar): the game world's climate-calendar foundation.
- [PBRT transmittance](https://www.pbr-book.org/4ed/Volume_Scattering/Transmittance): light attenuation.
- [GPU Gems water caustics](https://developer.nvidia.com/gpugems/gpugems/part-i-natural-effects/chapter-2-rendering-water-caustics): physical foundations and artistic approximation.

Teahouse compositing also draws on production accounts by [Ron Clements and John Musker](https://moviemom.com/interview-ron-clements-john-musker-little-mermaid-disney/), [Ed Jones](https://www.cartoonbrew.com/feature-film/who-framed-roger-rabbit-hits-30-a-look-back-at-ilms-astonishing-old-school-optical-vfx-158471.html), and [Disney's underwater-lighting account](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/news/creating-an-underwater-world-for-disneys-the-little-mermaid/). These explain techniques, not character identity or endorsement.
